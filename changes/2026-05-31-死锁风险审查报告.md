**修改原因：** 执行第4轮错误处理与可靠性审查，聚焦死锁风险  
**修改内容摘要：**

- 审查范围：`storage/database.py`、`storage/repository.py`、`memory/long_term.py`、`memory/consolidation.py`、`web/session.py`、`core/agent.py`、`core/inner_drive.py`、`core/tool_agent.py`、`core/dispatcher.py`、`memory/short_term.py`、`web/server.py`

- 输出修复优先级表（P0-P3），建议优先彻底 async 化以消除 `_run_sync` 死锁风险
